Jiam ("we", "us") operates the mobile game Arena Director: Esport Manager (package com.seolasoft.arenadirector, the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game processes and why, how long it is kept, and how you can control it.

Summary

The Game has no sign-up or login, and we do not ourselves collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Your game progress (saves) is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. Google AdMob processes the data below to show ads, and Google Play and RevenueCat process it to verify in-app purchases. The Game does not use any separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Google and are never shared with us. The RevenueCat app user ID is randomly generated and is not linked to your name or email address.

The Game does not collect: name, date of birth, email address (unless you contact us), phone number, postal address, contacts, photos or videos, microphone or camera data, precise (GPS) location, or a list of files on your device. The club and manager names you choose, your game progress, and the on-device error log (last 20 entries) stay on your device and are never sent to us unless you attach them to an email yourself.

2. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and do not provide it to third parties other than the service providers below, who process it to provide ads and in-app purchases in the Game.

3. Service providers

  • Google LLC — AdMob and User Messaging Platform: rewarded and interstitial ads, ad measurement, consent management. How Google uses data from partner apps
  • Google LLC — Google Play billing system: payments for in-app products, payment records, refunds. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: purchase receipt validation, managing and restoring purchased benefits. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. International transfers

Ad and purchase data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google and RevenueCat operate, transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections when ads are requested or purchases are made or checked. Transfers out of the EEA, UK, and Switzerland rely on lawful transfer mechanisms adopted by these providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. If you never make a purchase, no purchase records are sent to RevenueCat.

5. Advertising and your choices

The Game is free and supported by ads: rewarded ads you choose to watch, and interstitial ads shown at natural breaks after a match. Personalized ads are shown only if you consent or allow them on your device. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), a consent message appears on first launch, and you can change your choice in the Game under More → Settings → Privacy → Change ad consent. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. Ads still appear after opting out but are not personalized. The Game itself does not use cookies.

6. Deletion and your rights

  • Game data — delete saves in the Game under More → Save / Load, clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app. All game data on the device is then removed.
  • Advertising ID — reset or delete it in device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.
  • Purchase records — email us at the address below with your Google Play order number (starting with GPA). We will handle access or deletion of the matching record held by RevenueCat and reply within 10 days. Because there are no accounts, we cannot find records by email address alone.

See Data deletion for step-by-step instructions.

7. Security

  • All ad and purchase traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Game progress is stored only on your device; we do not operate a game server.
  • Access to the payment and purchase management consoles is limited to the operator, and we process only the minimum data needed.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the applicable age in your country). If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly. Purchases made by minors without guardian consent may be cancelled by the minor or guardian.

9. EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Jiam is the controller. Our legal bases are: performance of a contract for verifying and restoring the purchases you make; consent for ad storage and personalized ads (collected through the consent message); and our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention, and serving non-personalized ads. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time (More → Settings → Privacy → Change ad consent), and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

10. United States (California and other states)

In the past 12 months the following categories were processed: identifiers (advertising ID, RevenueCat anonymous app user ID), internet or app activity (ad interactions), purchase records, and approximate location (based on IP address). We do not sell personal information for money. Processing with advertising partners for personalized ads may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws; you can opt out through the Game's ad consent message or your device's ad settings. You may request to know, delete, or correct your information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
